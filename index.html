⚔ SPIRE ONLINE

MULTIPLAYER GAUNTLET RPG

🎮 MOVEMENT

Mobile: drag anywhere to move your hero.
Desktop: WASD or Arrow Keys.

⚔️ COMBAT

Your hero auto-attacks the nearest enemy.
Use skills to deal extra damage and survive.

✨ SKILLS

4 skills shown at the bottom of the screen.
Tap a skill button to activate it.
Desktop: press 1 2 3 4.
Unlock and upgrade skills with Skill Tokens.

📈 PROGRESSION

Kill Enemies Earn XP and Gold
Level Up +1 Stat Point per level.
Every 3 levels: +1 Skill Token
Stat Points Upgrade STR DEX CON INT WIS in the side panel →
Skill Tokens Upgrade skills up to Tier 3 in the side panel →

🏪 HUB

Between waves you enter the Hub.
Walk into glowing circles to open the Shop,
Skill Trainer, or Stat Mirror.
Walk to the north portal when ready for the next wave.

⚠️ WAVES

Every 5 waves: Mini-Boss appears.
Every 10 waves: Boss wave. Survive!
