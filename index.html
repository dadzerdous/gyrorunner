SPIRE

ONLINE · MULTIPLAYER SURVIVOR

🎮 MOVEMENT

Swipe anywhere on the screen to move in that direction.
Release to stop. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys.

⚔️ COMBAT

Your weapon auto-attacks the nearest enemy.
Tap the 3 skill buttons at the bottom to activate abilities.
Desktop: keys 1 2 3.

⚗️ ELEMENTS

Every class starts with a base element. Level up your Class Profile to unlock more elements to equip. Your equipped element combines with your weapon's element — matching elements cause OVERLOAD and two different elements create a COMBO.

🔥 Fire 💧 Water ❄️ Ice ⚡ Lightning ☠️ Poison 🪨 Earth 🌑 Dark ✨ Light 💫 Holy 💀 Necrotic 🌀 Void 🌪️ Wind

🤝 MULTIPLAYER COMBOS

When a teammate applies a status (e.g. Freeze) and you hit with a matching element (e.g. Fire), a CROSS-COMBO triggers for massive bonus damage. Coordinate your elements!

📈 PROGRESSION

In-Run Level Up Pick a card: class skill, weapon skill, item, or element
Weapon XP Use a weapon to rank it up — unlocks new skills
Class Profile Earn class XP after each run — unlocks elements and perks permanently
📖 Bestiary Fight enemies with different elements to reveal their weaknesses

🌑 CORRUPTION

Enemies spawn continuously and escalate every 45 seconds (TIER UP).
Every 5 tiers: Mini-Boss. Every 10 tiers: Boss wave.
Defeat the Boss to open the Portal and enter the Hub.

🏪 HUB

Walk into glowing zones to shop, upgrade stats, or train skills.
Walk north when ready for the next tier.
